Outage Workflow

How a down cell
becomes an impact report.

An interactive, zoomable walkthrough of the agentic outage analysis system. One recorded synthetic case is followed end to end: how each query is chosen, what data comes back, how the program turns it into state, text and a stopping decision. The actions are the recorded ones; the decision texts at each step are hand-written illustrations.
